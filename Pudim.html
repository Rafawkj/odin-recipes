Pudim Recipe

Pudding is a classic dessert known for its extremely creamy texture and delicate flavor. Traditionally made with milk, eggs, and condensed milk, it is baked in a water bath and covered with a glossy layer of golden caramel sauce.

Ingredients:

  • 1 cup of milk
  • 4 eggs
  • 1 can of condensed milk
  • 1 cup of sugar
  • 1 cup of water

Steps:

  1. Preheat the oven to 350°F (175°C).
  2. In a saucepan, combine the milk and sugar, and heat until the sugar dissolves.
  3. Remove from heat and stir in the condensed milk and eggs.
  4. Pour the mixture into a greased mold.
  5. Bake for 45 minutes or until a knife inserted into the center comes out clean.
