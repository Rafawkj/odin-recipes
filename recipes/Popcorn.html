Popcorn Recipe

Popcorn is a popular snack made from maize kernels that pop when heated.

Ingredients:

  • 1 cup of popcorn kernels
  • 2 tablespoons of oil
  • 1 teaspoon of salt

Steps:

  1. Heat the oil in a large pot over medium heat.
  2. Add the popcorn kernels and cover the pot.
  3. Shake the pot occasionally until the kernels stop popping.
  4. Remove from heat and season with salt.
